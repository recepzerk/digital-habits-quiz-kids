# Digital Habits Quiz for Kids Ages 9-12

A free and friendly quiz to help kids ages 9-12 understand their digital habits around screen time, gaming, YouTube, and online safety.

## What It Covers

10 questions across five areas:

- **Screen Time Awareness** — Do you notice how much time passes when you're on screens?
- **Gaming & YouTube Habits** — Can you stop when you need to?
- **Real Life Balance** — How do you balance screens with the rest of your life?
- **Online Safety** — Do you know how to stay safe online?
- **How Screens Make You Feel** — How does screen time affect your mood?

## Results

- **Digital Explorer** — You're learning, and that's great
- **Digital Thinker** — You notice a lot, keep going
- **Digital Champion** — You've got this
